# Law-school outcomes research notes

Historical research notes on law-school cost, debt, employment outcomes, reporting practices, and possible methods for finding graduates whose work was missed or misclassified by school surveys.

The material was assembled in the early 2010s and last changed in 2016. It is not maintained and should not be treated as current law-school employment, tuition, debt, accreditation, or labor-market data.

The original working document is preserved unchanged in [`notes.md`](notes.md). It contains long quotations, raw links, campus-proxy links, unfinished sentences, and unverified research leads.

## Main questions

- How many graduates obtained long-term jobs requiring a law degree?
- How did schools handle nonresponse and graduates working outside law?
- What did tuition and debt require graduates to earn?
- Could public professional records supplement school outcome surveys?
- How should salary, occupational fit, and nonfinancial forms of work be reported separately?

These are research questions, not conclusions established by this repository.
